Align now includes Music, Videos, Games, and Visual Art alongside Writing. This release also fixes artwork analysis failing because the selected Hugging Face model was unavailable through the user's enabled providers.

## Music: analyze a song and research its audience

Upload a song for local Essentia analysis, including tempo, key, energy, and changes across the recording. Explore platform and community routes based on your stated style, location, goal, and release stage.

Optional listener research uses artist names you enter to look up MusicBrainz identities and ListenBrainz aggregate listener counts and nearby artists. The audio stays on your computer. These are audience research leads; they do not identify individual fans or predict a song's plays.

## Videos: compare footage with public YouTube clips

Upload a video and supply search terms or a direct YouTube URL to compare its footage with public clips. Reports show the checked references, visual similarities, and available view counts, with saved results that survive an app restart.

Search coverage is YouTube only. Public view counts describe the reference posts. When no copy is verified, the report says **Virality not established** rather than treating a missing match as zero virality. A failed download or an interrupted analysis is reported explicitly.

## Games: analyze gameplay and find comparable titles

Upload gameplay from a released game or prototype to build a report with timestamped observations, inferred mechanics, audience archetypes, comparable games, and estimated platform fit. You can inspect the evidence behind the report and refresh comparable games without uploading the footage again or repeating GPU analysis.

Games uses a private function in your own Modal workspace and can reuse the Modal credentials saved for Brand Studio or Email Writer. Only sampled frames are sent for inference. Optional IGDB comparisons use Twitch application credentials entered under **Settings > Games — IGDB**; save and restart the app after setting them. GPU use is billed to your Modal account. Platform and audience estimates are research suggestions, not measured demand.

## Visual Art: review image labels and preference evidence

Upload a PNG, JPEG, or WebP artwork for optional subject and style suggestions. Review the labels, explore Artsy category links, and compare broad labels with aggregate human ratings from the PAMELA dataset. You can also select labels manually and run the matching step without Hugging Face inference.

Artwork analysis now uses **Qwen3.5-9B** and tries **Gemma 3 27B** when Hugging Face reports that no enabled provider serves the first model. The previous Qwen2.5-VL-3B selection caused the “requested model is not supported by any provider you have enabled” failure. Routing respects your enabled providers. Access, billing, rate limit, and provider errors now carry specific, redacted details.

PAMELA ratings describe other images with similar broad labels; they do not establish reactions to your upload or identify potential buyers. Automated artwork suggestions send a resized image to Hugging Face and use your account's inference credits. Live artwork inference with the user's provider settings has not been verified; routing recovery is covered by simulated provider responses.

## Verification

All **418 backend tests** pass. Electron's main process and renderer pass TypeScript checking, and the production renderer build passes. The bundled Essentia worker, music audience routes, and video report states and polling cancellation also pass their checks.

The dependency security scan still reports vulnerabilities in existing dependency versions. This release does not include a dependency security update. Semgrep was unavailable in the local release environment.

## Installing

The installers are unsigned. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper may warn when opening them. Windows users can use the app's update flow; macOS users should download the new DMG manually because unsigned macOS auto-updates are not supported.
