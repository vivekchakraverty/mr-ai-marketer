# Mr. AI Marketer 0.6.1

The Keyword Surfer collector arrived in 0.6.0 with one thing missing: the plan could not
use what it collected. This closes that gap.

---

## A collected run can be the basis of a plan

The Marketing Plan brief has a new **Keyword data** dropdown listing your finished
Keyword Surfer runs. Pick one and the SEO plan is built on those figures instead of
whatever the free tiers could estimate.

Previously the numbers were sitting one screen away from the section that needed them
most — the keyword sheet showed real volumes while the SEO plan was still written against
relative-interest buckets.

**Google Ads still wins.** An attached run is used *only* when the official tier returns
nothing, so this never overrides measured figures. The order is:

| | Source | Used when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Ads API | Credentials are configured and the call succeeds |
| 2 | **Your attached Surfer run** | Google Ads returned nothing |
| 3 | Autocomplete + Trends | Neither of the above |
| 4 | LLM estimate | Nothing else answered |

An attached run that has since been deleted is ignored rather than refused — the plan falls
back to researching its own keywords, exactly as before.

---

## The plan is planned around the numbers, not just given them

Related keywords used to reach the SEO stage as a bare list of names. Every figure Keyword
Surfer collects for them — volume, CPC, and how similar each idea is to its seed — was being
thrown away at the point it mattered.

They now travel with their figures intact, and the SEO stage is told what to do with them:

- **Cluster by similarity.** Terms close to the same seed usually belong on the same page.
- **Order by volume.** The 90-day content calendar leads with the terms most people search
  for, and each row carries its target term's monthly volume.
- **Read CPC as commercial intent.** A term people pay a lot per click for earns a landing
  page; a cheap informational one earns a blog post.

Each cluster now reports its **summed monthly volume** and names its highest-volume term, so
the plan can be checked against the data it was built from rather than taken on trust.

> Verified against a real collection: cluster totals of 25,800 and 4,200 matching their
> inputs exactly, landing pages assigned to the high-CPC transactional terms, and the
> calendar sequenced by volume.

---

## Paid ads use collected CPC when Google Ads has none

The ads plan's per-keyword Search CPC block previously used Google Ads figures or fell back
to a generic industry benchmark. It now uses collected per-keyword CPC in between — this
product's actual keywords beat an industry average.

It is labelled as the different thing it is, with an instruction to leave headroom in the
projection, rather than being passed off as Google's own auction data.

---

## Upgrading

Nothing to migrate. If you have no Keyword Surfer runs the dropdown reads *Research keywords
automatically* and the tool behaves exactly as it did in 0.6.0.
